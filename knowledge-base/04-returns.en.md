# Returns and complaints

## Damaged or wrong deliveries
Please note visible transport damage on the delivery note when you receive the goods.
Report damaged, missing or wrong items within 5 working days, with your order number and photos.
We replace or credit the items free of charge.

## Returns
- Unopened stock items can be returned within 14 days of delivery.
- Excluded: food, opened hygiene products, custom-printed items and special orders.
- Return shipping is free if the mistake was ours; otherwise we charge 15 EUR net per parcel.

## How to report a problem
Tell the support chat what happened and it will open a ticket for our customer service team,
or email service@muster-gmbh.example.
