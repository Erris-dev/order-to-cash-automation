# About Muster GmbH

Muster GmbH is a wholesaler based in Hamburg, Germany (Musterstraße 1, 20095 Hamburg).
We supply businesses only (B2B): hotels, restaurants, cafés, caterers, staff canteens,
care homes and offices. We do not sell to private consumers.

## Assortment
- Food & beverage dry goods: coffee, tea, sugar, breakfast buffet items, snacks
- Disposables & tableware: cups, lids, napkins, to-go packaging, tableware
- Cleaning & hygiene: detergents, dishwasher products, hand soap, paper towels
- Hotel room & guest amenities: toiletries, towels, table linen

## Delivery area
Germany, Austria and Switzerland. Other EU countries on request.
