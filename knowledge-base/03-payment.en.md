# Payment terms

- First order: payment in advance (bank transfer) or SEPA direct debit.
- After a positive credit check: invoice, payable within 30 days net.
- SEPA direct debit: 2% early payment discount (Skonto), debited 10 days after the invoice date.
- Credit cards are not accepted.
- All prices are net prices plus VAT (19%, reduced 7% for most food items).
- Invoices are sent by email as PDF (electronic invoice on request: XRechnung / ZUGFeRD).

Questions about a specific invoice, payment reminders or credit limits are handled by
our accounts receivable team; the support chat can forward your request.
