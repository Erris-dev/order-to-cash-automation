# Customer account and ordering

## Opening a customer account
Send us your company name, billing address, VAT ID (or trade registration) and a contact person
via the contact form on our website. Accounts are usually activated within 1 working day.

## How to order
- Online shop (24/7)
- Email: orders@muster-gmbh.example
- Phone during service hours

## Order numbers and status
Every order gets a number like S00012. Possible statuses:
- Quotation: not confirmed yet
- Confirmed: order accepted and being prepared for delivery
- Invoiced: the invoice has been issued
- Cancelled
