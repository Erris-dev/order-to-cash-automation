# Delivery and shipping

## Delivery times
- Hamburg metropolitan area: orders received by 2:00 pm on a working day are delivered the next working day.
- Rest of Germany: 1-2 working days.
- Austria and Switzerland: 2-4 working days. Deliveries to Switzerland include customs clearance; import VAT is charged separately.

## Minimum order and delivery costs
- Minimum order value: 500 EUR net per order.
- Free delivery from 750 EUR net per order. Below that, the delivery fee is 29 EUR net.
- Express delivery (same day, Hamburg only, order by 10:00 am): 59 EUR net.

## Tracking
Once an order is shipped you receive an email with the tracking number.
The order status (for example "confirmed" or "invoiced") can also be checked in the support chat
with your order number (e.g. S00012) and the email address of your customer account.
